# C041 tile-aligned cave steps — 2026-09-29

`FieldMapStructureTest` replaces the three smooth diagonal cave platform colliders from C018/C040. The existing PixelFantasy artwork remains unchanged. For each art cell, the highest occupied y at that x is its visible step; cells with consecutive x and equal y become one horizontal one-way tread. This produces 12 moss-slime crossing treads, 23 stone-golem crossing treads, and 9 upper-item landing treads (44 total). Each tread is a separate child GameObject with a rectangular `PolygonCollider2D`, `PlatformEffector2D`, and `FieldOneWayPlatform`. Its upper collider face is exactly at the associated tile cell's y+1. The former diagonal collider/effector/marker components were removed from their grouping parents, while parent identifiers, artwork prefab overrides and all unrelated scene objects remain unchanged.

This intentionally changes movement feel: a player can descend the steps and land from above; climbing the high side requires a jump rather than continuous uphill running. Adjacent treads differ by at most one world unit, below the authored jump's approximate 3-unit rise. S-drop still targets the contacted `FieldOneWayPlatform` child. A full keyboard traversal, including rolls between every tread, remains for manual review.

Verification: a source-vs-staged scene audit showed exactly nine removed diagonal components, 44 new tread GameObjects with 44 each of the required components, unchanged artwork and unrelated scene objects. Isolated Unity 6000.3.11f1 loaded all 44 treads. Play Mode physics passed landing from above, rising through from below and relanding on representative moss, stone and upper treads. A runtime-fitted player was captured with its foot on the moss tile's upper surface and the rendered image inspected. The validation used simulated physics/player placement, not a physical keyboard run or a player build. The copied editor logged its pre-existing SearchDatabase startup exception. No commit or push.
